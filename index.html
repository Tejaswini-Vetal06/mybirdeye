Platform Features

Identity Protected

Citizen identity stays hidden from authorities and other users, encouraging safe participation.

Location-Based Reports

Issues are accurately mapped, tracked, and visualized using geospatial intelligence.

AI Verification

AI validates reports, filters spam/duplicates, and scores severity automatically.

Safe Civic Participation

Encourages reporting of sensitive issues without fear of retaliation.

Human-in-the-loop

Relevant authorities manually verify AI-flagged reports before deploying resources.

Reward System

Citizens who make an impact get rewarded or acknowledged by anonymity choice.

Gamification + Civic Participation

Turn citizens into the city's sensor network while empowering existing city network infrastructure.
